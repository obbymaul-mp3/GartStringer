GartStringer

Supersaw trance, no drums.

Ethereal leads that go from euphoric to haunting, rolling trance bass, a gate and an arpeggiator, and a glitch engine, all on the M-VAVE FM-1. Built to sit beside your drum machine.

Try it in your browser

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. On this device you can still try GartStringer with sound.

The FM-1 restarted with GartStringer. You can unplug it now. Turn MASTER up slowly, then hold a key.

The install stopped. In most cases you can start it again.

Stopped before “Switching to update mode”? Nothing was written. Plug the cable back in and press Install again.

Stopped while writing, with the FM-1 still on? It waits in update mode with a black screen. Plug the cable back in and press Install again. Writing starts again from the beginning.

Did it switch off? Hold OCT− while switching on: update mode. Press Install again.

No flash at all, or a USB disk called WL82 UBOOT1.00? See “Something went wrong” below.

The sound

Five oscillators, six voices, 32 patches, in 3D

Leads from Sunrise Anthem to Ghost Signal, basses from Rolling Thunder to Hoover Bass, pads and plucks in between. Every patch is a starting point: nearly fifty parameters sit behind the pages. The screen is a little 3D world: towers rise and drift with the detune of your oscillator, every effect is a shape that turns and shows its setting, and the save page is a memory card with a turning shape in each slot.

Oscillators

Supersaw (seven detuned saws), hyper sync, PWM stack, roll bass and feedback, plus a sub. Detune, mix and colour are knobs.

Filter

A smooth state-variable filter: 12 and 24 dB low-pass, band, high-pass, and a vowel filter for choirs. Two envelopes, one LFO, glide, mono and legato.

Gate and arp

A 128-step note sequencer (eight bars) with accents, slides, swing, four directions and bar tools, a trance gate with fourteen patterns, and an arpeggiator with eight patterns, all locked to the tempo.

Controls

Four knobs per page

K1 – K4The four parameters of the page you are on
ALGORITHMScroll through that page’s other parameters
PRESETSChoose a factory patch
SELECTTempo
EDIT · SCL · ENV · LFO · FXOscillator, filter, envelopes, LFO and effects pages
ARPArpeggiator (press again: on / off)
SEQStep sequencer, 128 steps: ALGORITHM picks the step (a quick turn jumps a bar, as does OCT±), K1 note, K2 rest / note / accent / slide, K3 shifts it all, K4 rolls a new bar. Keys write the step. Press SEQ again for rate, length, swing, gate and direction, and again for bar tools: copy, clear, repeat, rotate.
GLOGlitch (press again: the trance gate)
HOMEPress again for the chord instrument: it shows the key and scale and the chord you play. ALGORITHM switches it on; K1 key, K2 scale (16), K3 layout (white keys = degrees, black keys = chord types, or chord buttons on the low keys; Lock latches), K4 voicing.
PLAYHold: the notes keep sounding. On the sequencer pages: run / stop. While it runs, the keys move it (key 13 is the root)
OCT− / OCT+Octave
SAVEEight slots for your own patches
27 keysPlay, from C3

Glitch

Hold REC and break it

Stutter, reverse, tape stop and drop-out, on the beat or by chance. Hold REC to fire it by hand; the screen turns red. Drive, bit crush, chorus, a tempo-synced ping-pong echo and a big reverb sit in the same chain.

Install

Your FM-1, in about a minute

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, plug the FM-1 in with a data cable and press Install above. Over the official firmware or any firmware that answers, it switches itself to update mode. Already on GartStringer? Hold OCT−, OCT+ and HOME for five seconds (or hold OCT− while switching on) to put it in update mode, then press Install.

        Something went wrong
        
An FM-1 that shows nothing and does not answer can still be written: hold OCT− while switching on. If a USB disk called “WL82 UBOOT1.00” appears, the chip’s own boot loader is running; the community unbricker at Quixotic7/MvaveFM1Unbricker restores it.

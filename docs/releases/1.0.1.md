## GartStringer 1.0.1

**The sequencer plays chords.** With the chords on, a step is a root: record by playing the chord keys (the chord sounds, its root is written), or write steps with the keys while stopped, and each step plays its chord as the chord page is set now. Change the voicing, the scale or the key and the whole sequence follows; a chord button held or locked shapes every step. With the chords off, a step is one note, as before.

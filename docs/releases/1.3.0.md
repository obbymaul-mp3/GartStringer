## GartStringer 1.3

**The FM-1 is now a USB audio input.** Plug it into a phone, an Android handheld or a computer and it appears as a recording device called "GartStringer In": stereo, 16-bit, 48 kHz. What it sends is the master output, exactly what the speaker and the headphone jack get: the volume knob sets the recording's level too, and the metronome click is in it when it is on. USB-MIDI works alongside it as before.

- Nothing to switch on: open a recording app and pick the FM-1 as the input.
- Saving a slot pauses the sound for a moment, and the recording with it.
- **If a computer or phone will not accept the new device:** switch the FM-1 off, hold REC, and switch it on while holding. The screen says USB AUDIO OFF and the FM-1 is the MIDI-only device it was in 1.2.1 until the next power-on. The installer page works in that state too.

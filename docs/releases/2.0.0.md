## GartStringer 2.0

**A new look.** The whole interface is redrawn in chrome on black, after the "metalheart" graphics of around 2000: liquid-metal shapes, thin technical lines, one signal colour. Nothing has moved: every page, knob and button does what it did, and your saved slots are untouched (this version changes only how things are drawn).

- **Each world has an emblem and a colour.** TRANCE: a star between two blades, pink. HOUSE: a heart with a ring through it, orange. TECHNO: a planet in a gyroscope, acid green. GARAGE: a crescent moon and a star, ice blue. The emblem is the home page's centrepiece; its glint swells with the sound and a glint lights for each voice.
- **Title screens.** The power-on screen is the world's emblem inside a ring of ticks with the name on a bar of chrome. Stepping to another world with ALGORITHM shows that world's title for a moment; any knob, button or key puts it away at once.
- **Chrome everywhere.** The sequencer's steps, the arpeggio's bars, the gate's slabs and the effects' shapes are 3D chrome with a dark horizon running through them. The filter, envelope and LFO curves are chrome tubes. The knob cards have chrome rims, and the name in the top bar is chrome lettering.
- **Clearer status.** A mode that is on (ARP, CHORD, HOLD, GLITCH) is a solid pill of the world's colour; one that is off is an outline.

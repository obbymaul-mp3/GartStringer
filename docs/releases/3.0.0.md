## GartStringer 3.0

Your saved slots are safe. Nothing about how a slot is stored has changed: this version reads every slot every earlier version saved, and a slot is rewritten only when you save over it. Slots saved here also load in 2.2, should you go back.

**A new look, all of it.** The chrome is gone. The screen is now drawn like a late-1990s fantasy game: indigo windows with parchment borders and hard shadows, gold for what is on, pink for what is alive or chosen, and names in a lettering drawn for GartStringer.

- **The title** at power-on: a castle at dusk, the name in gold, "Sir Obby's Synth" under it.
- **Each world is a place**, on the home page and on the card that shows when you enter it:
  - TRANCE: a castle on a hill at dusk.
  - HOUSE: a cathedral, with a checker floor, a rose window and organ pipes.
  - TECHNO: a torch-lit dungeon, with ghosts.
  - GARAGE: a shrine on an island, a sword in a stone under a broken arch.
  - AMBIENT: a moonlit forest.
  - BASS: a forge over lava.
- **The places answer your playing.** The castle's windows light, the cathedral's candles leap, the dungeon's ghosts bob (and their eyes turn pink), the shrine's gem and braziers flare, the forest's fireflies wake, and the forge spits lava. Different notes wake different things. At rest each still moves a little.
- **The four knobs are four rows**, K1 at the top to K4 at the foot, each with its name, a stepped gold meter and its value. A small sword marks the knob you turned last.
- **The sequencer's notes are gems**, a colour for each beat, higher for a higher note; an accent is pink; the playhead is a line with a pink diamond.
- **The effects pages draw what the effects do**: a gold note and its pink echoes, as many, as tall and as far apart as the echo is set, with the reverb's tail behind; and on the second page a wave cut into steps by the crush and shaken by the tape.
- **The matrix draws each route as a string** from its source to what it moves, with the amount on a tag; a live route's blood runs along it, backwards when the amount is negative.

**The sound, the patches, the sequencer, MIDI and USB are as in 2.2.** Nothing in the instrument itself was changed.

**The download page** has the same look.

## GartStringer 3.1

Your saved slots are safe: nothing about them changed. The new SYNC setting is kept in a small area of its own, apart from the slots.

**A SYNC setting for MIDI clock.** GartStringer has followed a clock on the MIDI IN jack or USB for a while; now you choose how.

- **Where:** the CLOCK page. Press SEQ until it comes (after TOOLS), or hold ARP for a moment on the arp's page. K1 is SYNC.
- **INTERNAL:** an outside clock, Start, Continue and Stop are ignored.
- **EXTERNAL:** only the outside clock is followed. With none coming, the sequence and the arp wait (the tempo shows `---`), and nothing is left sounding.
- **AUTO** (as shipped, and how it behaved before): the outside clock while it comes, the instrument's own tempo when it does not.
- The setting is kept through power-off. It is written a couple of seconds after you change it, once nothing is playing.

**The transport.**

- **Start** plays the sequence from step 1 on the next clock pulse.
- **Continue** now goes on from the step it stopped in (it used to start again from step 1).
- **Stop** stops the sequence and now also lets go a chord the arp was holding with HOLD.
- If the clock stops arriving for half a second: AUTO carries on at the tempo it had; EXTERNAL waits, with nothing sounding.

**On screen.** The top bar shows EXT, the measured tempo and a heart that beats with the clock while it is locked. The CLOCK page shows the tempo large, what is happening in words, and the 24 pulses of the beat as they arrive. The measured tempo now settles on the first full beat (it used to take four or five).

**With SYNC at INTERNAL, and with no clock connected, everything behaves as in 3.0.**

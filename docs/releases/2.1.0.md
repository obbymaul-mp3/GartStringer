## GartStringer 2.1

Your saved slots are safe: this version reads everything every earlier version saved, and never rewrites a slot until you save over it yourself.

**Two more worlds** (six in all; ALGORITHM on the home page steps round them).

- **AMBIENT** (a butterfly, mint): 24 slow patches. Pads, drones, bells and washed keys with long attacks and tails, big reverbs, tape wobble and drift. Slow tempos.
- **BASS** (a bolt, red): 24 loud ones. Distorted 808s that slide, reeses, growls, wobbles, donks, acid, crushed sines and a wall of saws, at 140 to 170 BPM.

**Knob recording.** Arm REC on the sequencer page and press PLAY, then go to any sound page and turn a knob: the movement is recorded into the pattern, a value for every step, and plays back each time round. One knob per pattern; a different knob replaces it. The steps show a point at the knob's height and the page names the knob. SEQ TOOLS, CLEAR turned left removes only the recording. It is saved with the slot.

**Scale lock.** On the chords page (HOME twice) ALGORITHM now steps OFF, CHORDS, SCALE LOCK. With the lock on, pick KEY and SCALE: any key that is not in that scale plays the scale's note just under it, so you cannot play a wrong note. The keys that are in the scale light up, the key's own note brighter. It stays on when you change patch.

**Drift.** EDIT twice opens a new PITCH page: DRIFT, OCTAVE, TAPE, GLIDE. Drift makes each voice's pitch, and the filter, wander slowly on its own path, so held chords move and no two notes are quite the same.

**Silent keys.** On the SAVE page, K3 is now KEYS: SOUND or SILENT. Silent, the keys still send notes over USB-MIDI but make no sound on the FM-1 (the sequencer still plays). The screen says KEYS SILENT while it is on, and it is back to SOUND at every power-on.

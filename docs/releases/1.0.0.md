## GartStringer 1.0

**It keeps time now.** The sequencer used to drop the few samples a step ran over, every step: about one sixteenth every 40 seconds at 128 BPM, so it slid away from other machines. Every clock (sequencer, metronome, gate, arpeggiator, echo) now counts in 1/256 of a sample and nothing is dropped, and the instrument measures its converter against the FM-1's 24 MHz crystal and corrects the tempo to it. The SAVE page shows the rate it measured.

**Two worlds.** Patches 1–32 are TRANCE; 33–48 are the new HOUSE bank. PRESETS runs through both, and ALGORITHM on the home page jumps to the other world. Each has its own colours and scene. The sequencer and your save slots are shared, and saves from 0.6 load as before.

**House.** Three new oscillators (FM KEYS, ORGAN, DONK), a TAPE control (wow, flutter, a darker top) on the second effects page, two pumps on the gate page (PUMP 1/4, PUMP 1/8: the sound ducks on the beat and swells back, like a sidechain), four house chord voicings (HOUSE 7, HOUSE 9, SHELL, PLANE M9), and a house fill in the sequencer's tools.

**A new look.** A pixel face throughout, glass cards for the knobs, a new title, the tempo and the world in the top bar.

Also: a key played just before the first downbeat of a recording now lands on step 1.

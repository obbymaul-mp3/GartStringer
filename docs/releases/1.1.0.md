## GartStringer 1.1

**Four worlds.** TECHNO and GARAGE join TRANCE and HOUSE, sixteen patches each, with their own colours and scene. ALGORITHM on the home page now steps round the four.

- **Techno:** an ACID filter (a sharp peak that an accented step opens further: write accents and slides in the sequencer), and three new oscillators: METAL (inharmonic FM), NOISE (pitched noise, for hiss and sweeps) and REESE.
- **Garage:** reese and organ basses, bell and organ stabs with the chords on, swing set from the start.

**MIDI clock.** Send clock into the MIDI IN jack, or over USB, and the sequencer, the arpeggiator, the gate, the metronome and the echo follow it; Start and Stop run the sequencer. The top bar shows EXT and the tempo it reads. Without a clock it runs on its own, as before.

**A new reverb.** The old one let a few low notes ring on long after the rest of the tail had gone, and a struck sound came back as separate echoes at first. The new one spreads the input before it enters, keeps the lows out of the loop, and sways four of its eight lines: a smooth wash with no pitch of its own. Patches with a lot of reverb will sound different: cleaner, less boom.

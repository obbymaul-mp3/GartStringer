## GartStringer 1.2

Your saved slots are safe: this version reads everything any earlier version saved and never rewrites it. New saves go to their own place in the flash.

**Four worlds.** TECHNO and GARAGE join TRANCE and HOUSE, with their own colours and scene. ALGORITHM on the home page steps round the four. 112 patches: 40 trance, 24 each of house, techno and garage.

- **Techno:** an ACID filter (a sharp peak that an accented step opens further), and three new oscillators: METAL, NOISE and REESE. Screech, hypnotic stabs, an offbeat sub, 16th-note acid.
- **Garage:** reese, organ and wub basses, a pure sub, bell and brass stabs with the chords on, swing set from the start.
- **House:** tech and bass-house basses, a house piano, an afro pluck, disco strings.
- **Trance:** a pumped supersaw wall, a melodic pluck, psy bass, a hard lead, a rave piano.

**Steps that repeat, steps left to chance.** SEQ, SEQ again: the STEP page. REPEAT makes a step sound two, three or four times inside its own time. CHANCE makes it sound every time, or 75, 50 or 25 % of the time, so a line changes a little on each pass. SOUND lets a pattern keep a sound of its own: A the bass, B the chords.

**The notes stay when you change the sound.** Once you have written or recorded anything, turning PRESETS changes the sound and leaves your patterns and tempo alone.

**A performance page.** HOME, HOME again. MACRO is one knob over the whole sound: BUILD (a rise: filters open, the lows thin out, reverb and echo grow), WASH (the sound dissolves into its reverb and echo), DARK (the top closes down) or CRUSH (drive and bit crush). DJ FILT: left of the middle closes a low-pass, right raises a high-pass. PUMP: how hard the sound ducks on the beat. ALGORITHM switches pattern here. None of it changes the patch; turn it back and the sound is as it was. The top bar shows FX while any of it is on.

**MIDI clock.** Send clock into the MIDI IN jack, or over USB, and the sequencer, the arpeggiator, the gate, the metronome and the echo follow it; Start and Stop run the sequencer. The top bar shows EXT and the tempo it reads.

**A new reverb.** The old one let a few low notes ring on after the rest of the tail had gone. The new one is a smooth wash with no pitch of its own. Patches with a lot of reverb sound cleaner, with less boom.
